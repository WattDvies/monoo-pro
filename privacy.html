Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Monoo Pro is a tasks, calendar, focus, habits and notes app for Windows and Android. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Using the app without an account

Everything you create stays in a file on your own device. Nothing is sent to us.

Accounts

If you create an account, we store your name, email address and a securely hashed password with our sign-in provider (Supabase). If you use “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app. We use these only to sign you in and show your name in the app.

Your tasks and notes

When sync is on, your tasks, notes, habits and settings are encrypted on your device with a key made from your sync password before they are uploaded. We store only this encrypted data and cannot read it.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone except the service providers needed to run sync (Supabase for storage and sign-in, Google for Google sign-in). Data from Google is used only to sign you in, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Optional features

Features you turn on yourself — such as email-to-task, calendar subscriptions, Notion or webhooks — connect directly from your device to the service you choose, using the details you enter.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data on a device from the app’s settings or when uninstalling. To delete your account and all synced data, open an issue at github.com/WattDvies/monoo-pro/issues and we’ll remove it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: github.com/WattDvies/monoo-pro/issues.
